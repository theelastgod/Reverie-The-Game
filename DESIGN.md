# Reverie — playable chapter redesign

The master brief remains the creative and systemic contract. The current prototype does not yet satisfy its five-hour campaign, readable action combat, or coherent city targets. Hundreds of globally triggered plaques are not authored hours. Stage numbers measure changes shipped, not game completeness.

## Preserve

The cybernetic angel, the city already after its ending, the Fourfold, named people with conflicting needs, grief, and extraction that changes the world. Preserve Imagine art as a shared visual vocabulary. Keep server authority, fair combat and every real-value bridge disarmed.

## Change

Build one convincing opening chapter before expanding the map or claiming campaign duration. Aim for a measured 15–20 minute first playthrough: arrive, learn to evade and interrupt a clerk, meet Nara, choose a node's fate, bury what that choice costs, hear the conflicting accounts, and reach the threshold. The campaign must create obligations and consequences rather than reward repeated reading.

The playable surface is architecture, paths, cover, people and danger. Illustration stills belong in location portraits and story presentation, not framed postcards scattered over the floor. Gold and sky mark the sacred; lavender belongs to the machine; acid stays in the interface. Use fewer materials with intentional boundaries. New bitmap art should extend the existing family and answer a specific gameplay need.

Dodge is a deliberate input with a brief window and a recovery period. Walking is not immunity. Heavy interrupts; light commits; enemy anticipation must be readable before damage. Hit feedback should follow the server result. Guests get the same basic control grammar as Angels.

## First implementation

- Personal opening NPC placement and dialogue protect new arrivals from another character's later story.
- The first Safety reading always contributes to that character's weather account.
- Shift plus movement requests a server-controlled dodge with collision, duration and cooldown. Restraint modestly extends its window. No token or serial damage modifier.
- Reduce floor materials and floating plaques. Reuse authored stills in field notes.
- A reusable intake shift gives every arrival access to a readable first fight. Hits, misses and interrupts all recover; participation belongs to each character.
- PvP requires both Angels to flag explicitly. Truces protect both directions; the practice ground is safe for people. Hot streets and season progress do not choose combat for the player.
- Nara's recorder makes the first resource decision part of a burial: preserve a voice and use cloth, or dismantle copper for coffin bindings. Personal decisions persist while the public memorial changes. No additional numeric reward is created; the original finite yield economy remains separate.

## Next acceptance gates

1. Instrument and play the opening from a fresh session. Combat and the node choice must occur naturally before the guest threshold; neither should depend on another player leaving shared enemies or resources intact.
2. Clerk anticipation, recovery, health feedback and reusable encounters are implemented and tested. Verify their timing and legibility in rendered play.
3. The first memorial decision now changes Nara's burial and the shared recorder. Extend its consequences into later party choices; preserve both routes and do not require extraction to unlock the story.
4. Test the chapter at laptop size with readable sprites, controls, near-object labels, dialogue and stable frame pacing. A screenshot alone does not establish 60fps.
5. Extend this chapter structure to the other movements only after the opening is playable without a wiki and its duration is measured.
