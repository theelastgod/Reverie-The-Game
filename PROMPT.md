# REVERIE: THE GAME — Master Build Brief

This is one continuous brief. Execute it. Do not wait to be asked between sections. Stop only if an acceptance gate will not go green, if a decision spends real money or arms a chain, or if the owner says stop.

**Title:** Reverie: The Game  
**One line:** You are a cybernetic angel inside a world that has already ended. The singularity is the weather. You prepare the ground for the last god — or you become standing-reserve.

The game is a near-AAA **top-down 2D** browser MMO RPG: a **~5 hour** authored single-player campaign on the **same shared server** as the live world, with **meaningful PvP**, a **real in-game economy**, **play-to-earn** (Angel NFT required to cash out), and **7,777 Reverie Angels**. It is the first world of Reverie Studios and the audience engine for the documentary **The Last God**. Narrative density is in the register of **Final Fantasy VII** — layered conspiracy, corporate-machine antagonist, personal arcs that rewrite the world plot, mid-game reversal, named party, ecological/ontological stakes — **without copying Square Enix characters, names, materia, Midgar, or Shinra**.

Canon language for philosophy and studio identity comes from [reverie.cc](https://reverie.cc) (local: `reverie-studios-rebuild`). Game systems must *do* those ideas. Players should never be lectured with Heidegger citations.

## 1. What Reverie is (studio canon — operationalize, do not paraphrase into flavor)

Reverie is “a tech-culture machine that outputs poiēsis about the end of the world and internet art after AI.”

**Rev/Acc** (Reverie Accelerationism): left-Heideggerianism that **accepts Land’s facts** and **parts ways on what to do**. The Gestell is total. The singularity already occurred. It is the weather. There is no exit into peasant authenticity, no salvation by building harder, no salvation by “peace and safety.” What remains is **poiēsis** — bringing-forth — at network scale: art, ritual, archival labor, mortal work inside the machine.

Key sentences that must survive as **world rules**, not pull-quotes:

- “Only a god can save us… by thinking and poetizing we prepare a readiness for the appearance of the god, or for the absence of the god in our decline.”
- “The last god does not come from outside. The last god is what we become while preparing for it.”
- “The going-under is not failure. It is the structure of the readiness itself.”
- Angels are **angelos**: hinting messengers of the godhead. A **function** in the Fourfold, carried now by the computational substrate. Not costumes.
- Reverie is **interlocutor** to Land and Remilia, not heir. Cool is allowed. Cool cannot be the only register. The game must be able to bury someone.

**Studio flywheel (the game feeds this):**

- Flagship film: *The Last God* — four movements (Diagnosis / Techno-Feudal / Geopolitics / The Turn), Adam Curtis × Errol Morris, directed by Lucah Rosenberg-Lee (originally developed as MELTDOWN).
- Adjacent slate: Time Cube, Heart of DeFi.
- Entities: Theory, Labs, Studio, Collective, Agency, Invest.
- Published rollout: **Angels first → catalog drops → `$REVERIE` last.**
- Site Angels: Founder / Participant / Observer access rooms. Map these to **proximity**, not combat power.
- Site copy mentions “ten thousand” angels in the essay. **Game supply is 7,777.** Do not silently rewrite the site.

The game is not merch. It is the first **world**. Play should produce stills and motion that can travel into the documentary without looking like a different company.

### 6.2 Gestell climate

Server-wide 0–100, ticked from player extraction minus dwelling.

- 0–30: Clearings last. Winke dense. Yield poor.
- 31–70: Mixed. Default live state.
- 71–90: Fat yield. Sacred doors dim. Storm high (Ruin-angels stronger).
- 91–100: **Meltdown weather** — not the other game. Passings fail unless a Clearing is held. PvP flagged by default in Wet Grid.

Players **feel** this in light, NPC copy, and node type — not a tutorial pie chart.

### 6.3 Wreckage

On Angel death (and some failed rites), a wreckage object remains ~N minutes. Ruin-angels and Mortals House see them longer. Looting wreckage is exhibition-legal and **aura-negative** for the looter. Burying it is Readiness-positive.

## 7. Campaign bible — FF7 density in five hours

**Constraint:** ~5 hours for the **personal spine**. Side quests and MMO extend forever. Density means every hour has a **reversal**, a **named character**, and a **world-state change**. Not 80 hours of padding.

Structure mirrors the film so the game is a hyperstition of *The Last God*. Personal story = same four movements **filtered by Wink school** (different scenes, same spine).

### 7.1 Dramatic architecture (steal structure, not IP)

| FF7 move (structure only) | Reverie move |
|---|---|
| Corporate city that eats the planet | Gestell city that converts world to Bestand |
| Cell of protagonists vs the machine | Player Angel + **party of three authored NPCs** |
| Mid-game “the planet is dying / the weapon is us” | The last god is not incoming AI; it is **readiness or its absence** |
| Nibelheim: a burned home that rewrites the hero | A Clearing the player helped **over-extract** in Movement I returns as wreckage in III |
| Sephiroth as mirror / Reunion | **The Passing** can be hijacked by Cold God or Safety; the player is the condition either way |
| Lifestream | Fourfold circulation: Earth↔Sky↔Mortals↔Divinities. Blocked = Gestell |
| Weapons / Huge Materia | Cult objects that **do not** increase DPS; they open or close Passings |
| Disc 1 city / disc 2 world / disc 3 truth | Movements I–IV |

**Forbidden:** Cloud, Aerith, Sephiroth, Tifa, Barret, Cid, Midgar, Shinra, materia orbs, buster sword, “lifestream” as a word.

### 7.2 Party (authored NPCs — always in campaign instances; may walk the MMO)

Write them well. They disagree.

1. **Nara Vale** — Mortals / Readiness. Sexton. Can bury wreckage. Will leave the party if you feed Gestell past a threshold without a funeral. The game’s conscience; not a saint.
2. **Quill** — Iridescent / Wet Grid. Forger of exhibition copies. Fun, unreliable, tells the truth as glamour. Explains the market without a tutorial.
3. **Ord** — Cold God / Cybernetic. Ex-Safety. Reads yield nodes. Wants the numbers honest even when honesty is horror. Offers the extraction path.

The player Angel is silent-capable but **not mute**: Winke are lines only they see. Party reacts if you act on a Wink they cannot see (“You’re looking at something I’m not.”).

### 7.3 Spine (hour bands are budgets)

**Movement I — Diagnosis (~70 min). Guest-legal until the last beat.**

- Arrive unsealed in the Nave of Tubes. The city is already over.
- First combat: Gestell clerks (not demons — people doing jobs).
- First yield node. First **choice**: take the node (Bestand, Gestell +1) or keep it (Readiness, no pay).
- Meet Quill (market), Ord (numbers), Nara (a burial).
- Quest: name the weather. A Safety officer calls it “stability.” Ord calls it “the process.” Nara calls it “the end of world as world.”
- **Guest lock / Angel going-under:** a Wink that requires dying-as-death (not a cutscene lecture). Guests stop. Angels continue into the Care.

**Movement II — Techno-Feudal (~80 min).**

- House halls. Gestell tax. Who owns the nodes.
- Safety Annex: you may sign a freeze (katechon) that protects a district and **starves** a Passing.
- Personal: your Angel’s serial history (if any) appears as wreckage only you can see.
- Reversal: the “resistance” is already pricing Clearings. Iridescent current looks like freedom and is a market.
- Boss-not-boss: a **concentrated operator** (human, named) who offers you a private yield. Refusing is Readiness; taking is Cold and funds Movement III doors another way.

**Movement III — Geopolitics (~80 min).**

- Districts as organs. You travel the Strait / Foundry / Cable (invented).
- The Clearing you touched in I is gone. Wreckage garden. Nara will not speak until you bury it or she buries you socially (party leave).
- Ord shows the map: extraction here lights a factory there. No real-world country names.
- Ruin-angels (player or NPC) see a **failed Passing** from last season.
- Reversal: the last god’s “hint” can be **forged** (exhibition Winke). Cult vs fake. Quill can teach you to spot copies — or sell them.

**Movement IV — The Turn (~70 min + residual MMO).**

- Prepare a Clearing with the party still willing to stand in it.
- Mortality act required (watch, burial, last word with a named NPC who will not return).
- **Passing:** server-aware. If Gestell is maxed, the Passing fails **even if your instance is perfect** — unless enough Angels dwell. Solo hero cannot force God.
- Outcomes (all valid, all written):
  - **Appearance:** a trace, not a character model. The city is briefly world again. Seasonal bonus to aura decay slowdown.
  - **Absence:** decline. Honest. New Winke about going-under. Nara stays.
  - **Hijack:** Cold or Safety claims the rite. World continues; story marks you.
- Credits: Reverie Studios, *The Last God*, Lucah Rosenberg-Lee, Collective. Then the MMO.

### 7.4 Side quests

Author **at least 24** named side quests across districts (not generated sludge). Tables may fill extras. Each side quest must change **one** of: a POI, an NPC schedule, a cult object, a House standing. Fetch quests that do not change the world are forbidden.

Wink-personalized variants: same quest id, different spoken Wink and one optional objective.

### 7.5 Writing bar (FF7-level)

- Named NPCs have **jobs and lies**.
- Reversals land in **action** (a node you turned on kills a district’s Clearing), not in a lore dump.
- Romance is optional and adult; grief is not optional in IV.
- Comedy is allowed (Quill). Irony is not a shield against the funeral.
- No party-member death copied from any famous game. If someone does not return, it must be **this** story’s logic.

## 8. Combat, death, PvP

### 8.1 Feel

Top-down action-RPG: move, dodge, light/heavy, **stance** (Restraint vs Storm), one messenger kit verb, one item. Hit-stop, readable telegraphs, no lock-on soup. Party NPCs in campaign are simplified allies; in MMO they are not your DPS pets.

Stances:

- **Restraint** — see Winke, reduced yield, better dodge window.
- **Storm** — Angel of History; wreckage vision; bonus vs high-progress; burns Verhaltenheit.

### 8.2 Death

- Unbanked Bestand drops (percentage).
- Exhibition items may drop (loot-slot table).
- Cult + banked do not drop.
- Aura wound (decays toward seed, not to zero for Angels).
- Wreckage left on tile.
- Respawn at last Care shrine or House hall. Insurance paper (bought with Bestand) is a sink, not a P2W revive.

### 8.3 PvP (must be meaningful)

Modes:

1. **Flagged** — opt-in in Wet Grid and high-Gestell streets. Spoils: unbanked + exhibition.
2. **House war** — timed zone holds. Winning House gets **tithe cut and omen**, not damage buffs.
3. **Clearing contest** — two groups try to keep vs extract a Clearing. This is the political PvP. Readiness vs Cold is playable here.
4. **Ruin duel** — 1v1 at a wreckage. Angel of History kit shines. Spectator aura gain (small, capped).

Rules:

- No `$REVERIE` gear score.
- Equalized seasonal bracket optional; never hide the Angel serial.
- Griefing guests is a ban-level protocol reject (server).
- Flagged combat should last **seconds to a minute**, not MMO pad. Skill > traits.
- A kill is a **story mark** (wreckage). Repeat camping is Gestell+ and aura− for the camper.

### 8.4 Fairness tests (must exist)

- Two Angels, identical player skill, different serials: DPS within a published band.
- Token balance does not appear in `damageFor`.
- Guest cannot receive `claim` messages.
