REVERIE:
THE GAME

You are a cybernetic angel inside a world that has already ended. The singularity is the weather. You prepare the ground for the last god — or you become standing-reserve.

Play Controls

Guests play Movement I in the browser. No wallet, no download.

Cold · pressurized · operational

01

Campaign

Four movements on the live server. Diagnosis, the techno-feudal city, the Organs, the Turn. A party that can leave you. Guests lock at the going-under: a guest cannot prepare the ground.

02

MMO & PvP

Houses of the Fourfold. Clearing contests and Passings that need more than one Angel. Flagged fights in the Wet Grid and the Organs that take spoils from people, not a faucet. Wreckage stays on the map.

03

Bestand & $REVERIE

Bestand is earned in-world and every earner has a sink. Only a linked Angel can bank it. The token never buys combat. Serials, aura and items change perception, verbs and style — never a number that decides a fight.

    Non-negotiables

    What the server will not let anyone buy.

    1. The server owns every number. Combat, loot, aura, Bestand, Winke, claims, PvP, weather, Passings. The client sends intents and renders.
    2. The token never buys combat. $REVERIE, serials, aura and items never touch damage, drop rates, win chances, quest completion or House rank. Traits change perception, verbs and style.
    3. Only a linked Angel earns. Guests play Movement I and lock at the going-under. Guests cannot claim, flag, see Winke, or enter the Care, the Clearing or the Organs.
    4. Every earner ships a sink. No unbounded faucet. The claims desk ships disarmed: no mint, no real-value settlement.
    5. Death is real. Unbanked Bestand and exhibition items drop, aura is wounded, wreckage stays on the map. Cult objects and banked value never drop.
    6. A solo hero cannot force a Passing. At meltdown the Clearing must be held by dwelling Angels.
    7. PvP needs both flags. No truce, not on the practice ground. Camping the same body costs the camper.
    8. No yield promises. Anywhere.
